E-Commerce System Requirements

The e-commerce system contains customers with a unique identifier, date of birth,
address, and name. A customer can have multiple credit cards associated with them,
and a credit card can be shared by multiple customers.

Products can be viewed and purchased by customers. Each product has a unique
identifier, name, price, quantity, and an availability flag. Staff members can add,
edit, and remove products from the system, and can change the quantity or price of an
existing product. Staff members can also mark a product as unavailable, and every product update is
logged in the inventory update history.

Customers can add products to a temporary cart and continue adding items until they
checkout. During checkout, the system records a purchase containing the customer ID,
credit card number, total price, purchase date, and each purchased item with its
quantity and unit price. Customers can view their purchase history after checkout.

The system also supports switching between customer and staff roles.
Customers can view and edit the credit cards associated with their customer ID, and
staff members are restricted from editing another customer's card records.
